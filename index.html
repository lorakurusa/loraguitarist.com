Welcome to Lora Kurusa's guitar portfolio — a reflection of artistic excellence, refined musicianship, and a dedicated career in classical guitar performance and education.

Lora Kurusa is a Hungarian classical guitarist, educator, and performance coach based in Auckland, New Zealand. With over 12 years of experience performing and working within the classical music industry, she has developed a career that brings together professional performance, education, and a deep interest in the psychology of performing under pressure.

Lora has performed extensively across New Zealand and Australia as a soloist and ensemble musician. Her musical achievements have been recognised through numerous scholarships and opportunities to participate in prestigious guitar festivals and programmes throughout Australasia. At 15, she received a scholarship to study at the Elder Conservatorium’s Open Music Academy in Adelaide, and in 2020, at 17 years old, Lora was awarded First Prize in the Youth Section of the Biennial New Zealand International Classical Guitar Competition. Lora has completed a Bachelor of Music in Classical Guitar Performance at the University of Auckland, and at 21, she made her concerto debut performing Rodrigo’s Fantasia para un gentilhombre with the New Classics Sinfonia.

Lora is proud to be supported by two sponsors whose work is closely connected to her career as a musician. She is a sponsored artist of Domenic Roscioli, an Italian-Australian classical guitar luthier who handcrafted a beautiful custom instrument specifically for her. Her guitar, the Lora Model, was created through a close collaboration between luthier and performer and has become an important part of her artistic identity. She is also a sponsored artist of Dr. Tom Nailcare, whose specialised products support the nail care essential to classical guitar performance.

Alongside her performance career, Lora has developed a particular focus on performance coaching for musicians. Drawing from more than a decade of firsthand performance experience, she works with musicians to understand and navigate performance pressure, develop reliable performance routines, and build greater confidence and consistency on stage.

Her approach is grounded in the belief that performance confidence is not something you either have or don’t have — it is a skill that can be developed. Rather than teaching musicians to eliminate nerves, Lora helps them learn how to perform effectively while nerves are present, using practical strategies, deliberate exposure to pressure, and thoughtful reflection.

Lora is also a Suzuki-trained classical guitar teacher and maintains a private studio, working with students of all ages and abilities, from complete beginners through to advanced players and Grade 8+ students. She has a strong track record of preparing students for graded examinations, with students achieving High Distinction results in their guitar exams. Her teaching combines strong technical foundations with musical expression, thoughtful practice strategies, and a personalized approach to each student’s goals. She also supports students preparing for performances, auditions, and other musical opportunities.

Whether performing on stage, teaching in the studio, or coaching musicians through performance challenges, Lora’s work centers around helping musicians become more confident, capable, and expressive performers.

Programs
8-Week Performance Intensive

The Calm Under Pressure Method

Ready to take your performance skills to the next level? This personalised 8-week program is designed to help you reach your performance goals and become your own performance coach.

Together, we build your performance routine, develop practical tools to stay calm and focused, understand your body’s response to pressure, and progressively expose you to performance situations so you can build genuine confidence through experience.

You’ll receive weekly 1:1 calls, personalised homework, performance challenges, and ongoing 24/7 support throughout the program. Every element is adapted to your individual challenges, so you’re never left wondering what to work on next.

By the end, you won’t simply know how to perform under pressure — you’ll understand how to coach yourself through it, giving you the tools to keep improving long after the eight weeks are over.

Online Performance Community

Aoraki Performance Academy

A place to perform, grow and connect.

Aoraki Performance Academy is an online performance opportunity for classical guitarists to perform, receive feedback, and connect with other musicians in a supportive environment. Co-founded by Michael Stoop (New Zealand performer, educator and composer) and Lora Kurusa, Aoraki is built on the belief that performing is a skill in its own — one that deserves to be practiced.

We hold a small online performance class every three weeks, giving each player dedicated time to perform and receive support.

The Foundation — Perform

For those who want to perform regularly and build experience. Includes a recording of your performance to keep and reflect on.

The Elevation — Perform + Feedback

For those who want to actively improve their performance. Includes everything in The Foundation, plus personalized feedback from Michael and Lora. Michael focuses on technique and musicality, while Lora focuses on performance skills and confidence.

The Summit — Perform + Feedback + Coaching

For those wanting the most personalized support. Includes everything in The Elevation, plus a complimentary 1-hour individual lesson with Michael and Lora, combining technical guidance with dedicated performance coaching.

Incanto Wedding Music

Lora Kurusa & Emma Ma

Classical Guitar & Violin

Dolce Aspro

Lora Kurusa & Darren Breeze

Dolce Aspro is a classical guitar and violin duo born from a long-standing friendship between Lora and violinist Darren Breeze, which has naturally blossomed into a professional musical partnership. Having performed together in a number of concert settings, the duo brings both musical chemistry and a shared love of expressive performance.

Darren is an accomplished violinist with extensive experience in orchestral, chamber, and contemporary performance. Most recently, he has performed with the New Zealand National Youth Orchestra, following four years as Assistant Principal First Violin of the Auckland Youth Orchestra. He has also received first prize at the Te Awamutu Performing Arts Competition, showcasing his versatility and experience as a performer.

Their repertoire combines classical and romantic music with vibrant gypsy-inspired and upbeat repertoire, creating a programme that can move seamlessly between intimate, lyrical moments and energetic performances.

Classical Guitar Duo

Across Strings

Lora Kurusa & Michael Stoop

Across Strings is a classical guitar duo featuring Lora Kurusa and award-winning guitarist, composer, and teacher Michael Stoop. Lora and Michael first met while studying Classical Guitar Performance at the New Zealand School of Music under Dr Jane Curry, and their musical paths have continued to cross through competitions, summer schools, and ensemble performances.

Michael has established himself as both a performer, teacher and composer, with his original works published by SOUNDZ and performed by musicians throughout New Zealand. He released his debut album Landscapes, featuring entirely original compositions for solo guitar, and has received recognition including the New Zealand Aspiring Guitarist Award and prizes at the Biennial New Zealand International Classical Guitar Competition.

Now based in Christchurch, Michael and Lora have developed Across Strings as an inter-island collaboration celebrating the versatility of the classical guitar. Beyond performing together, the pair also co-founded Aoraki Performance Academy, a coaching space helping fellow guitarists build confidence and skill on stage.

Classical Guitar & Flute

Nocturno Duo

Lora Kurusa & Yune-Sang Yune

Nocturno Duo brings together classical guitar and award-winning Korean–Kiwi flautist Yune-Sang Yune. Yune-Sang made her professional solo debut with the Auckland Philharmonia Orchestra and is a Gold Medal recipient of the Manhattan International Music Competition.

The duo most recently performed together in a candlelight concert, following previous performances around Matakana and Auckland. Their repertoire explores modern, Spanish, romantic, and classical music, combining the warmth of the guitar with the expressive colour of the flute to create an intimate and atmospheric performance.
